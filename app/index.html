    GoodRx

    GoodRx blocks automated reads, and its partner API's terms forbid showing its prices next to any other source. So it isn't a column here. The MCP server's check_quote tool compares it instead: you bring the price GoodRx showed you, and it tells you whether anything here beats it.

    Strengths and quantities are our choice of a common fill; neither published list gives one. Cost Plus prices come from its public pricing API, with fees and standard shipping included. Team Cuban Card is Cost Plus's discount card. It shows only when a participating pharmacy is within 30 miles. "Pharmacy paid" is the median NADAC across generic manufacturers. It's a yardstick, not a price you can buy at.

    Source code, method and the terms behind every source: rx-compare/ in the hbschlac/hbschlac GitHub repo.
